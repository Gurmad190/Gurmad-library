    Receive full access

    Once payment is confirmed, your full PDF and Word files are sent to your WhatsApp and email — the complete book, ready to read on phone, tablet, or computer.

    Marka lacagta la xaqiijiyo, faylasha buuxa ee PDF iyo Word ayaa laguu soo dirayaa.

Help — Good to know

What is the Complimentary Reading Edition?

On each book's own page, Inside the Book (Gudaha buugga) shows its Selected Pages (Bogag la soo xulay) — title pages, contents, and the Featured Chapter (Cutub la soo xulay). You can also download the same pages as the Complimentary Reading Edition (Nuqul akhris oo bilaash ah) PDF, free to keep and share. The rest of the book opens when you Get Full Access (Hel buugga oo dhammaystiran).

Which language are the books in?

Both. Every concept is explained in English and in natural Somali side by side — technical terms are defined in English with a full Somali explanation of what the idea means, not just a word-for-word translation.

Can I get all three books together?

Yes — the Complete AI Learning Collection (Taxanaha buugaagta waxbarashada) brings all three full editions together for $18.00 instead of $25.00 bought separately (Save $7, 28%). Or get any single book on its own — each book is complete by itself, with its own subject and its own page, and new books will be added to the library over time.

How do I receive a book?

Digitally. Each book is delivered as a PDF plus an editable Microsoft Word (DOCX) file, sent to your WhatsApp and email after payment is confirmed. There is no shipping wait.

Buying for a school or class?

Add a note in the My Library form with the number of students or teachers. Gurmad Educational Hub works with schools and training groups across Somalia and can arrange group delivery and support.

When do coming-soon books arrive?

They are in preparation now. Open a coming-soon book's page, copy our WhatsApp number (+252 907 327 475) and message us the book's name — we will reply on WhatsApp as soon as that book is released, with its final contents and page count.

Publish a Book

Share your book with readers through Gurmad Library. Anyone may submit — every book is reviewed by Gurmad before it goes live.

Buuggaaga ku daabac Maktabadda Gurmad. Qof kasta wuu soo gudbin karaa — buug kasta Gurmad ayaa eega ka hor inta aanu soo bixin.

How publishing works · Sida ay u shaqeyso

Anyone may submit a book to Gurmad Library — students, teachers, writers, and professionals. Every book is reviewed by Gurmad before publishing: nothing goes live automatically, because we check the book, its description, and its price first.

Paid books are sold the same way as the current books — readers order on WhatsApp and pay by EVC Plus, eDahab, or bank transfer, and the full book is delivered to them. Free books are downloadable by anyone once they are approved.

  1. Fill in the submission form.
  2. Press “Submit for Review” — WhatsApp opens with your details already written; just press send there.
  3. Gurmad reviews your book and replies to you on WhatsApp.
  4. Once approved, your book appears in the library, in its category, with its own page.

Qof kasta wuu soo gudbin karaa buug — arday, macallin, qoraa, ama xirfadle. Buug kasta Gurmad ayaa eega ka hor inta aan la daabicin; midna si toos ah uma soo baxo. Buugaagta lacagta leh waxaa loo iibiyaa sida buugaagta hadda: akhristuhu wuxuu ku dalbadaa WhatsApp, wuxuuna ku bixiyaa EVC Plus, eDahab, ama wareejin bangi. Buugaagta bilaashka ah qof kasta wuu soo dejisan karaa marka la ansixiyo ka dib.
